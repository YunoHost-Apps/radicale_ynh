Your calendars and address books were converted to the Radicale 3 format. If some data needed adjustments (renamed collections, new UIDs for duplicates...), the upgrade log lists them, and they are also saved in `__DATA_DIR__/radicale1-backup/migration-report.txt`.

A copy of the original Radicale 1.1 data and configuration is kept in `__DATA_DIR__/radicale1-backup`. Once you have checked that your clients synchronize correctly, you can delete it: `sudo rm -r __DATA_DIR__/radicale1-backup`.
